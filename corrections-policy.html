Corrections Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Our Commitment

Accuracy is the whole basis of a review site. Despite careful research, mistakes can happen — a specification changes, a model is revised, a price range goes stale. This policy explains how to report an error and what we do when you do.

How to Report an Error

Email us at nazmul.i.hridoy@gmail.com and include:

  • The URL of the page with the error.
  • What the page says, and what you believe is correct.
  • A source for the correction, if you have one (for example, the manufacturer's specification page).

You can also reach us through our Contact page.

What Happens Next

  • We review every correction report against the original sources for the guide.
  • If the report is right, we correct the page promptly — normally within a few business days of verification.
  • Significant corrections (a wrong specification, a changed pick, a safety-related update) are made visibly: the corrected text is updated in place and the page's "Updated" date is revised so readers can see the guide has changed.
  • Minor fixes such as typos are corrected without a formal note.

What We Will Not Do

We do not alter a verdict, remove a product, or soften a criticism simply because a manufacturer or reader disagrees with it. We correct facts; opinions and rankings change only when the underlying facts or our scoring criteria justify it, under our Editorial Policy.

Safety Issues

If a product in any guide becomes subject to an official recall or safety notice, we update the guide to say so and link to the official notice as soon as we become aware of it. If you spot a recall we have missed, please email us — safety corrections are our highest priority.
