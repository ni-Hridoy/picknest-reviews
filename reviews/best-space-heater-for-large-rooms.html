What Is a Space Heater, and How Does It Heat a Large Room?

A space heater is a portable electric heater that warms one room rather than a whole house, and there are three ways it does the job. Ceramic fan heaters pass air over a heated ceramic element and blow it into the room — fast, directional, and the only type that can spread heat across a large space quickly, especially with oscillation. Oil-filled radiators heat sealed diathermic oil inside steel fins, which radiates warmth passively — slow to start, silent, and even. Infrared heaters warm people and objects directly rather than the air, which feels immediate but covers a narrow area. For a large room, the ceramic tower with oscillation is the practical default, and the oil-filled radiator is the specialist for bedrooms and long, quiet sessions.

The running cost is the same for all three at full power, because the physics is fixed: 1,500 W at the U.S. average of about $0.17 per kWh costs roughly $0.26 an hour. The difference is how often the heater runs at full power. A thermostat and an ECO mode that steps power down once the room reaches temperature cut the average draw sharply — which is why the Dreo's ECO mode and the De'Longhi's ECO Plus matter more than their headline wattage. Close the doors, draw the curtains, and let the thermostat do its job; heating a room with the door open to the hallway is heating the hallway. If the room is genuinely large — an open-plan living and dining area over 300 sq ft — one 1,500 W heater will not hold the temperature on a cold night, and two smaller heaters placed at opposite ends beat one big one in the middle. Space Heater Safety: The CPSC Guidance

Space heater safety is not a footnote — it is the first thing to get right, because the CPSC's numbers are stark. Portable heaters, including electric space heaters, are involved in an average of 1,600 fires per year, resulting in an average of 70 deaths and 150 injuries annually from 2020 to 2022. The Commission's guidance is specific and worth following exactly: keep portable heaters at least three feet away from drapes, furniture, bedding and other combustible materials; plug electric space heaters directly into a wall outlet, never into an extension cord or power strip; place the heater on a stable, level, hard surface where it cannot be knocked over; turn heaters off when sleeping or leaving the room; and never place a heater close to a sleeping person. Keep children and pets away, and make sure working smoke alarms are installed on every floor, outside all sleeping areas and inside each bedroom.

When you buy, choose a heater that has been tested to the latest safety standards and certified by a nationally recognized testing laboratory — all three picks here are ETL-listed or equivalent — and look for a guard around the heating element, a tip-over switch and overheat protection. Never use a portable electric heater in a bathroom unless it is specifically designed for that use, and never leave any space heater running unattended in a confined space around infants or people with reduced mobility. The 3-foot rule is the one to remember: three feet of clear space in every direction, every time. Buying Guide: What to Check Before You Buy

  1. Measure the room honestly: the Dreo covers 100–270 sq ft; the Lasko lists up to 150 sq ft; the De'Longhi suits small to medium spaces. Over 300 sq ft, plan on two heaters.
  2. Pick the heat type for the room: ceramic fan tower for a living room you want warm fast; oil-filled radiator for a bedroom you sleep in.
  3. Check the thermostat range: the Dreo adjusts 41–95°F in 1-degree steps; the Lasko 60–85°F; the De'Longhi up to 82°F.
  4. Demand the safety trio: tip-over shutoff, overheat protection, and certification from a nationally recognized testing laboratory.
  5. Use the timer: the De'Longhi's 24-hour timer compensates for its slow warm-up; the Dreo's 1–12 hour timer and the Lasko's 7-hour timer stop all-night running.
  6. Buy in Black Friday week: space heaters are among the deepest-discounted winter items in late November — the Lasko has been seen under $60.
